# Price Finder

My app helps indie game studios decide pricing by analyzing and comparing prices of similar games.

> A course project for **MIST 4600: Computer Programming in Business** at the University of Georgia's Terry College of Business (Fall 2026).

## Problem
Small game developer studios might be unsure about what to price their new game on launch. This app helps them analyze studios who have released similar games based on genre, ratings, etc. This data gives them a better idea of what similar games were priced at upon launch. 
Who your user is, what decision they have to make, and why it's hard without your app. (2-3 sentences)

## What it does
My app answers three questions:
1. **Market price:** What do games in the studio’s genre cost, and has that changed over the years? 
2. **Ratings:** In that genre, do more expensive games get better or worse ratings? 
3. **Revenue:** Which price band earns the most estimated revenue in the genre

    
## Data
The [Steam Games Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/artermiloff/steam-games-dataset) on Kaggle, collected in March 2025. The dataset is shared under the MIT License by its creator, artermiloff. This repo uses `steam_games.csv`, a smaller file made for the course: the 1,000 games with the most reviews.
